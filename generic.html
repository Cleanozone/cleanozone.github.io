Our Car Care
Services

Professional car wash, deep cleaning, detailing, polishing, and rim painting.
We treat every vehicle with the care and attention it deserves.

🚗 Premium Car Wash

Our premium car wash service goes beyond a simple rinse. We use high-quality foam cannons, pH-balanced soaps, and professional techniques to safely remove dirt and grime without damaging your paint. Every wash includes:

  • High-pressure pre-rinse to remove loose dirt
  • Foam cannon application with pH-neutral soap
  • Hand wash with microfiber mitts
  • Thorough wheel and rim cleaning
  • Complete drying with premium microfiber towels
  • Tire shine application

🧼 Deep Cleaning (Interior)

Restore your car's interior to like-new condition. Our deep cleaning service removes stains, odors, and embedded dirt from every surface:

  • Complete vacuuming of seats, carpets, and trunk
  • Hot water extraction for carpets and upholstery
  • Leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Dashboard and console detailing
  • Window and mirror cleaning (interior & exterior)
  • Air vent and crevice cleaning
  • Sanitization and odor elimination

✨ Professional Polishing

Remove swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation to restore that showroom shine. Our multi-stage polishing process includes:

  • Paint inspection and decontamination
  • Clay bar treatment to remove bonded contaminants
  • Compound stage for scratch removal
  • Polish stage for gloss enhancement
  • Finishing stage for mirror-like reflection
  • Paint sealant or wax application

🔧 Complete Detailing (Full Package)

The ultimate transformation for your vehicle. Our complete detailing package combines everything:

  • Everything from Premium Car Wash
  • Everything from Deep Cleaning
  • Everything from Professional Polishing
  • Engine bay cleaning and dressing
  • Headlight restoration (if needed)
  • Trim and plastic restoration
  • Final inspection with premium wax/sealant

🎨 Rims Painting & Customization

Transform the look of your vehicle with professional rim painting. Whether you want matte black, gloss, or a custom color:

  • Thorough rim cleaning and degreasing
  • Surface preparation and sanding
  • Primer application
  • Base coat in your chosen color
  • Clear coat for protection and shine
  • Available finishes: Matte, Gloss, Metallic, or Custom

🛡️ Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting protection for your vehicle's paint. Ceramic coating creates a hydrophobic layer that repels water, dirt, and UV rays:

  • Paint correction preparation
  • Surface decontamination and panel wipe
  • Ceramic coating application (1-5 year options)
  • Hydrophobic protection
  • UV resistance to prevent fading
  • Easier cleaning for years to come
